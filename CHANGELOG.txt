FIELDIV-ENGINE - CHANGELOG
==========================

Alpha 0.1 - revision 9 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- New enemy: the King's Field Kraken (the octopus). Until now the level used KFIV's snake in its place. It uses
  its real King's Field model and texture, with a skeleton (body, six legs, two long arms) and its own animations:
  idle, light and heavy hit, death, an ink spit at range and a whip with both arms up close. It behaves like
  KFIV's Dark Flower (it stays in place). None of KFIV's enemies are removed: it is added as a new type.
- Untested on a real PS2 or emulator yet: report if it crashes the level, looks wrong or never attacks.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 8 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- Secret walls and doors stay open while you are near: they only close once you walk about 8 m away (they closed
  after a moment even with you standing next to them).
- New EXTRAER_CHR.bat: copies KFIV's enemy files from your ISO into chr_kf4.zip, to study them for porting the
  King's Field Kraken (the octopus that guards the secret door).

Alpha 0.1 - revision 7 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- Enemies use KFIV's dynamic enemy slots (like the original map 008): each enemy type is loaded when one of its
  enemies is near and dropped when none is, so a ported level is no longer limited to 4 enemy types. The test level
  now keeps 7 KFIV enemy types instead of turning the extra ones into others.
- Collision packed much smaller: flat rectangles on the same plane are joined and triangles are chained into strips
  (2.6 MB -> 2.0 MB on the test level). The map now leaves about 0.8 MB free.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 6 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- Secret walls: fixed the dark strip on the floor of the opening (the underside of the wall was left on the
  floor), and the floor and frame around the wall take the colors of the floor and walls next to them.
- Secret walls: object brightness recalibrated from in-game screenshots.
- Secret walls: flush with the wall on both sides (the original model's lower slab sat 31 cm back on one side),
  and they rise exactly their own height (no more seeing the empty slot above them).
- Secret walls: the seam line on the ceiling around the wall is gone.
- Secret walls: the slot the wall rises into is closed (sides and top), so the sky no longer shows through it.
- Secret walls: opening one shakes the camera slowly, rumbles the controller and plays a sound.
- Secret walls: the rising wall has a bottom and a top face (open, it looked like two loose sheets); the
  bottom no longer disappears when you stand right under it.
- Secret walls close again when you walk away, like KFIV's own doors.
- Doors now use their real King's Field model (wooden knocker doors, big grey doors, stone door) and rise like the
  secret walls, with a sound. Their objects are packed much smaller so everything still fits in the map.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 5 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- New vertex lighting for King's Field interiors: about four times brighter than before, floors lighter than
  walls and walls than ceilings, soft shadow at the foot of the walls, subtle variation and a warm tone.
  Torches and the fountain still add their own light on top.
- Secret walls take the vertex colors of the walls right next to them, so they blend in from both sides.
- Secret walls are now as thick as the wall they sit in (they looked sunk into their opening).
- Fixed light specks on diagonal shore floors: polygons crossing a 64-texel boundary used a clipped texture area.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 4 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- Secret walls: removed a small white spike at the foot of the wall (a hidden piece of KFIV's slab).
- Secret walls: no more flickering floor where the wall's floor meets the floor next to it.
- Secret walls: darker, closer to the walls around them.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 3 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- Secret walls: the sides of the opening stay in place when the wall rises (you could see through the frame),
  and its light matches the walls next to it.
- Enemy damage cap now only applies to the snake; every other enemy hits as in the original game.
- The map editor is now Field4Editor: English by default, with an English/Español button.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 2 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- New layout: FIELDIV-ENGINE.exe (menu launcher) + engine\ folder with the tools + this CHANGELOG.txt.
  Put your King's Field IV ISO next to the .exe (or inside engine\).
- Secret walls: the rising wall keeps KFIV's own slab collision and opening (rotated and scaled to the
  King's Field wall), and rises the full height of the wall. Brighter, so it matches the walls around it.
- All .bat files use Windows line endings.

Alpha 0.1 (2026-10-26)
----------------------
First public release.

Map generator / King's Field port
- Ports King's Field levels into King's Field IV (PAL, SLES-50920), replacing map 099 (New Game).
- Real King's Field geometry and textures, with collision built from the models.
- Decor imported from the original MO.T models: barrels, tables, tombs, lamps, the healing fountain.
- Secret walls with the original King's Field model; doors as a rising slab with the original door texture.
- Healing fountain: HP only, with KFIV's own fountain script and effect.
- Breakable barrels; torches and lamps add a soft warm light; bluish fountain room.
- Snake damage capped at about 10 per hit (ported map only).

HUD and gameplay patch
- HP number inside the orb.
- Cross HUD: magic on top (icon, name, MP cost), consumable at the bottom (icon and count).
- Weapon EXP bar and level in the equipment screen; weapons level up at 70 and 140 hits.
- Optional twin-stick controls; starting gear in engine\equipo_inicial.txt.
- PARCHE_HUD.bat: HUD and/or controls patch for your own ISO, without the map generator.

Known issues
- Secret walls and doors are new: report anything that looks off.
- An enemy between you and an object takes the X button (the game's own priority).
