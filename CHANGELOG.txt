FIELDIV-ENGINE - CHANGELOG
==========================

Alpha 0.1 - revision 19 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- New enemy: the King's Field giant snail (the one with the spotted body and the stone shell). Like the Kraken
  it keeps its real model and texture with a skeleton and animations made for it (crawl, bite, hits, death) and
  behaves like KFIV's slime. Level 0 has 7, in their original spots (they were bats before: that King's Field
  class was mislabeled "S-Knight" in the table).
- Several new enemy models can now go in the same ISO, each in its own place.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 18 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- Enemy choices made on the "Enemigos del nivel 0" map page are saved in engine\reparto_nivel0.json, and the
  port uses them: each marked enemy keeps the type picked there, and the Kraken goes where it was placed.
- This revision includes the first set of choices (10 enemies changed, Kraken moved).

Alpha 0.1 - revision 17 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- KFIV keeps at most 5 enemy types loaded at once. With every type loading from 45 m, the caves of level 0 had
  more than 5 around and the Kraken never got a slot, so it never appeared. Enemies now load from 30 m, fish
  from 22 m, and the Kraken from 60 m so it takes its slot first.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 16 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- Fish swim deeper (2.6 m under the surface) and never go more than 2.4 m from where they were born, so they
  no longer float out of the water when they chase you.
- Level 0: where the original had its land "Kraken" class there are now slimes (c0002); skeleton spots are
  mostly c0004 with some skeletons (c0050) and plants (c0000).
- The single Kraken now stands right in front of the secret wall (two tiles out, on the open side).
- Doors: the floor of the King's Field door model stays on the ground instead of rising with the door, and
  the extra bottom face is only added where there is a clear slab (secret walls).
- Re-port the level (JUGAR_KF2.bat).

Alpha 0.1 - revision 15 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- King's Field level 0 gets its enemies from a hand-made list instead of the automatic look-alikes:
  c0004 is the most common one, slimes (c0002) with one poison slime (c0003), skeletons (c0050) and the plant
  (c0000) where the skeletons were, bats (c0010) in the caves with a mushroom (c0087) where several bats meet,
  and a single Kraken, the one next to the secret wall.
- Fish in the water: KFIV's Seafang (c0043) as a new enemy type, with 30 HP (two hits), about 10 damage per
  bite and 5 EXP. They always respawn and swim 1.6 m under the surface, where the original level had its water
  enemies (they were spiders before).
- The Kraken no longer spits (no enemy spit shows its texture on this map); it only whips.
- Re-port the level (JUGAR_KF2.bat) to get the new enemies.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 14 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- Kraken: its ranged attack is now a water jet (the Aqualizard's). The Dark Flower's spores it used before draw
  with the flower's own texture, so they came out as a blank sheet.
- Kraken: its PS1 textures are enlarged 2x with smoothing and turned into one 256-colour texture, closer to how
  KFIV's own enemies look, and its vertex colours are darker underneath and near the ground for some volume.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 13 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- Found the real cause of the black screen: the map's texture section has to start on a 16-byte boundary, and
  the longer wall scripts of revision 8 left it 4 bytes off. The generator now always aligns it (and the sound
  bank). The close-when-you-walk-away script is back on: walls and doors stay open while you are near and close
  about 8 m away.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 12 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- The level loads again: the new close-when-far script of revision 8 was what hung it (found with the test
  builds). Secret walls and doors are back to closing like in revision 7 (KFIV closes them itself) until the
  cause is found; PROBAR_CIERRE_guion0.bat and PROBAR_CIERRE_abierta.bat are the two tests for that.
- The Kraken stays in.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 11 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- Fixed the level not loading (black screen) since revision 8. The new close-when-far script of the secret walls
  and doors used the script's "set" word with a wrong length, so whenever the game looked for a script further
  down it lost its place and looped forever while loading the map. The Kraken was not the cause.
- The generator now walks the whole map script the way the game does and, if any label would be lost, leaves the
  wall scripts out instead of producing a map that hangs.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 10 (2026-10-26)
------------------------------------
- Fix attempt for the level not loading after revision 9 (black screen): the Kraken's model no longer goes
  right after the map in the disc image (that could push the map to a place the PS2 cannot read); it gets its
  own spot, and if there is none the Krakens become snakes again instead of hanging the game.
- PROBAR_SIN_PULPO.bat: builds the same level with snakes instead of the Kraken, to tell whether it is the cause.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 9 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- New enemy: the King's Field Kraken (the octopus). Until now the level used KFIV's snake in its place. It uses
  its real King's Field model and texture, with a skeleton (body, six legs, two long arms) and its own animations:
  idle, light and heavy hit, death, an ink spit at range and a whip with both arms up close. It behaves like
  KFIV's Dark Flower (it stays in place). None of KFIV's enemies are removed: it is added as a new type.
- Untested on a real PS2 or emulator yet: report if it crashes the level, looks wrong or never attacks.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 8 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- Secret walls and doors stay open while you are near: they only close once you walk about 8 m away (they closed
  after a moment even with you standing next to them).
- New EXTRAER_CHR.bat: copies KFIV's enemy files from your ISO into chr_kf4.zip, to study them for porting the
  King's Field Kraken (the octopus that guards the secret door).

Alpha 0.1 - revision 7 (2026-10-26)
-----------------------------------
- Enemies use KFIV's dynamic enemy slots (like the original map 008): each enemy type is loaded when one of its
  enemies is near and dropped when none is, so a ported level is no longer limited to 4 enemy types. The test level
  now keeps 7 KFIV enemy types instead of turning the extra ones into others.
- Collision packed much smaller: flat rectangles on the same plane are joined and triangles are chained into strips
  (2.6 MB -> 2.0 MB on the test level). The map now leaves about 0.8 MB free.

Alpha 0.1 (2026-10-26)
----------------------
First public release.

Map generator / King's Field port
- Ports King's Field levels into King's Field IV (PAL, SLES-50920), replacing map 099 (New Game).
- Real King's Field geometry and textures, with collision built from the models.
- Decor imported from the original MO.T models: barrels, tables, tombs, lamps, the healing fountain.
- Secret walls with the original King's Field model; doors as a rising slab with the original door texture.
- Healing fountain: HP only, with KFIV's own fountain script and effect.
- Breakable barrels; torches and lamps add a soft warm light; bluish fountain room.
- Snake damage capped at about 10 per hit (ported map only).

HUD and gameplay patch
- HP number inside the orb.
- Cross HUD: magic on top (icon, name, MP cost), consumable at the bottom (icon and count).
- Weapon EXP bar and level in the equipment screen; weapons level up at 70 and 140 hits.
- Optional twin-stick controls; starting gear in engine\equipo_inicial.txt.
- PARCHE_HUD.bat: HUD and/or controls patch for your own ISO, without the map generator.

Known issues
- Secret walls and doors are new: report anything that looks off.
- An enemy between you and an object takes the X button (the game's own priority).
