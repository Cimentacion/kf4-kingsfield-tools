FIELDIV-ENGINE - CHANGELOG
==========================

Alpha 0.1 - revision 40 (2026-10-28)
------------------------------------
- The King's Field AI is now general and also used by the skeletons, green slimes and Head Eaters. Each one is
  set up like the KFIV monster it is built on (hits taken, death, walking, animations) and then decides like
  King's Field, with the weights and distances of its level 0 class (engine/kf1_ia_datos.py):
  - idle, wander (walks to random points around where it appeared), move towards you (from its start distance;
    keeps following up to a longer one), guard and up to three melee attacks;
  - each decision every possible action draws a random number up to its weight and the highest wins; a melee
    attack weighs less when you are farther than where it lands, and moving weighs double right after an attack;
  - after an attack, or when you are too close, it backs away if its KFIV model has a walk-back animation
    (otherwise it stays facing you).
- Skeletons: wander, come at you from 16.5 m, guard when you swing at them from close, double slash and thrust
  with King's Field reach and angles (never less than what the KFIV soldier had). Green slimes: bite all around.
  Head Eaters: bite from 4.8 m and back off.
- Kraken also has its King's Field idle action now; baby Krakens whip with their real weights (100, 100, 80).
- More King's Field action types decoded in docs/kf1_ia.md (wander, the skeleton's double slash, thrust and
  guard, how moving backs away).
- Baltails and the fish keep the KFIV bat and Seafang AIs (they fly or swim).
- Without the known executable each monster falls back to the AI of its KFIV base monster.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 37 (2026-10-28)
------------------------------------
- Kraken and baby Krakens have their own AI now, a new KFIV enemy type (0x53) written in C (engine/ia/kf1_ia.c)
  and compiled into the executable as an extra ELF segment after the end of the program (the heap starts after
  it). It decides like King's Field: each decision, turning towards you and each whip in reach draw a random
  number up to their King's Field weight and the highest wins.
- Kraken: doesn't walk, turns towards you from 12.2 m (keeps doing it up to 14.6 m), three whips like King's
  Field: 7.1 m (52 degrees), 7.1 m (stronger) and 9.5 m (44 degrees). It keeps seeing you even if something is in
  the way (the beetle AI stopped attacking when the line of sight was blocked).
- Whip hits now reach as far as the whip is chosen from (before, the choice used the reach plus the body radius
  plus 1 m, and the hit only the reach).
- Baby Krakens: crawl up to 2.6 m from you, whips at 3.3, 3.3 and 4.1 m.
- If the executable isn't the known SLES_509.20, they fall back to the beetle AI.

Alpha 0.1 (2026-10-26)
----------------------
First public release.

Map generator / King's Field port
- Ports King's Field levels into King's Field IV (PAL, SLES-50920), replacing map 099 (New Game).
- Real King's Field geometry and textures, with collision built from the models.
- Decor imported from the original MO.T models: barrels, tables, tombs, lamps, the healing fountain.
- Secret walls with the original King's Field model; doors as a rising slab with the original door texture.
- Healing fountain: HP only, with KFIV's own fountain script and effect.
- Breakable barrels; torches and lamps add a soft warm light; bluish fountain room.
- Snake damage capped at about 10 per hit (ported map only).

HUD and gameplay patch
- HP number inside the orb.
- Cross HUD: magic on top (icon, name, MP cost), consumable at the bottom (icon and count).
- Weapon EXP bar and level in the equipment screen; weapons level up at 70 and 140 hits.
- Optional twin-stick controls; starting gear in engine\equipo_inicial.txt.
- PARCHE_HUD.bat: HUD and/or controls patch for your own ISO, without the map generator.

Known issues
- Secret walls and doors are new: report anything that looks off.
- An enemy between you and an object takes the X button (the game's own priority).
