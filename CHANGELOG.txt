FIELDIV-ENGINE - CHANGELOG
==========================

Alpha 0.1 - revision 70 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- Streaming gated by doors, as in KFIV's own maps. A King's Field door on the border between two streamed maps now
  belongs to the map you reach it from first, counting from the start (double doors move both leaves). It is closed
  when you arrive, even if the map behind it is not loaded yet. Before, it belonged to the map of its tile, so
  without that map there was no door and no floor. This covers the first cave room of level 0 (door 16,17 / 17,17)
  and the room in front of the link tunnel to level 1 (33,4 / 33,5).
- On such a door, the forced wait for the map behind it (record byte 0x39) is only on the door tiles themselves and
  on the copied floor behind. Before the door, the game only requests the load, so it keeps loading while you open
  the door. In the load simulator, forced waits drop from 7 to 4, and the first cave room has none.
- Copied floor behind a map border always forces the wait for the neighbour.
- The partitioner prefers to cut through King's Field doors: a crossing through a door tile costs nothing.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 69 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- Bridges and walkways of the ported King's Field levels now have floor collision. They are MO.T objects (190-192,
  196-198), not grid tiles, and decoration objects had no collision, so you fell through the level 1 bridge. A
  floor face of those objects, or a grid floor more than 1 m above the floors around it, gets collision when it
  can be reached walking from a walkable floor (step up to 1 m, tile to tile). Roofs are still not reachable and
  stay without collision.
- NPC dialogue follows the game language. Each line keeps the same message ids in every language and each DAT gets
  its own font.txc: Spanish uses the translation, the other languages use the original English text of the
  European King's Field (TALK0.T, read with OCR and corrected by hand: kf1_dialogos_en.py). Before, every language
  showed the Spanish text.
- The level 0 fisherman uses his own King's Field model (MO.T 67, seated on his rock with the fishing rod). It is
  mounted on the bones of KFIV's NPC 101, at real size (2048 PS1 units = 2.5 m) and rigid, as in the original.
  No chr file was free, so it replaces chr/c0101.chr, which only this NPC uses in the mod.
- Ported models: when the PS1 textures do not fit a 256x256 sheet at double size, they are kept at their own size.
  The texture that gets replaced is the model's 256x256 one, and the template block is one that draws with it.
  KFIV NPCs carry several textures.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 68 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- Water is no longer copied into the border of neighbouring maps: each streamed map carries only the water of its
  own tiles. Both loaded maps draw their water again, as the neighbour pairs of every flag block now include
  group 4. In revision 66 only the current map's water was drawn, so the sea disappeared when seen from a cave
  mouth that belongs to the cave's map. No water is drawn twice, so the doubled green band does not come back.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 67 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- The forced wait for the neighbouring map (record byte 0x39) now starts 2 tiles before the door instead of 1. If
  the neighbour is still loading, the game stops you before the end of the copied floor, which reaches 2 tiles
  past the border. In the link tunnel to level 1 you could walk off it and fall.
- A large floor polygon crossing the border between two streamed maps now goes into both, not only into the map
  that owns the tile under its centre.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 66 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- Water and sky come only from the current map. Each streamed map copies the geometry around its border, so both
  loaded maps drew the transparent water where they overlap. That doubled water showed as a bright green band
  with a straight edge, appearing and disappearing as the neighbour loaded and unloaded. Water records now have
  their own flag group (4), the sky keeps group 3, and neither is in the neighbour pairs of any flag block.
- Every map with an open-air or cave-mouth floor carries its own sky. Before, maps with little open air relied on
  the neighbour's sky, which vanished at the cave mouth when the neighbour unloaded.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 65 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- Fixed the background colour indoors. The game picks the fog colour (header 0x50 + 4n, also the screen clear
  colour) from byte 0x3B of the record under the player: it reads 0x38 as a u64 and keeps bits 24-25. KFIV's map 8
  does this. The engine was setting bit 24 of the flags at 0x3C (byte 0x3F), which no original map uses, so the
  background was always colour 0, the bluish outside one. Unloaded parts showed as flat bluish patches even deep
  inside. Covered floors now set byte 0x3B = 1 and get the black background.
- Each streamed map now also carries the floor collision of the copied geometry around it, up to 2 tiles past its
  border. Before, while the neighbour was not loaded, you could see that floor (e.g. the start of a bridge) and
  fall through it.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 64 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- The cave mouth band (outside fog, sky visible) is now 1 tile (2.5 m) instead of 3. A few metres into a cave,
  unloaded parts still showed in the bluish outside fog colour.
- Every covered floor, the cave mouth included, now uses the black background colour (fog colour 1). Only open-air
  floors keep the bluish one, and there the sky covers it.
- Water records no longer use MAP_RECORD_CURRENT_BLOCKS. With the light of the floor under the player, the sea
  showed a bright green band with a straight edge. It is lit and fogged as before revision 62.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 63 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- The cloud sky no longer shows through unloaded rooms indoors. The sky record now has its own flag group (3), and
  covered floors more than 3 tiles from the open air use a flag block that does not draw it. This is how KFIV's
  own indoor maps behave. Before, any room not loaded yet showed the bluish sky behind it, even deep in the cave.
  Now the background there is the indoor fog colour (black).
- The floor you stand on decides the fog, background colour and sky: open air (light fog, sky), covered within
  3 tiles of the open air, e.g. a cave mouth or porch (light fog, sky), or inside (close black fog, no sky).
  Each piece of geometry goes in the same record as its floor's collision, so maps have fewer records than before.
- Neighbouring pieces start loading 14 tiles (35 m) from the door instead of 10, beyond the indoor fog. In the load
  simulator, the gap at the stairs up to level 1 is gone.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 62 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- Fog by where you stand, as in KFIV's own maps: every record of the streamed maps has MAP_RECORD_CURRENT_BLOCKS
  (0x10), so it is drawn with the fog (and light) of the record under the player, faded over 30 frames when it
  changes, instead of its own. Inside, everything you see gets the close black fog; outside, everything gets the
  light one. Before, each wall had its own fog, so from the same spot some walls were flat fog-coloured patches
  and others were not.
- Covered floors within 3 tiles (7.5 m) of the open air count as outside for the fog: from under a porch you look
  out with the light fog, not a black shore.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 61 (2026-10-29)

- The cloud sky only goes in maps with at least 30 % of their tiles in the open air: inside, whatever is not loaded

Alpha 0.1 - revision 60 (2026-10-29)

Alpha 0.1 - revision 59 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- Streamed build much faster again (here 1 minute; it was 3 in rev 57 and about 10 before):
  - each level's geometry is built once (it was rebuilt every time the build switched from one level to the other,
    up to 5 times, 30-60 s each, "in case the editor kept state": checked, the maps come out the same except the
    random turn of the items on the floor);
  - neighbours are found by touching tiles instead of nearest-distance searches, and tiny loose pieces are merged
    by distance between centres (that search alone took 40 s).

Alpha 0.1 - revision 58 (2026-10-29)

Alpha 0.1 - revision 57 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- Building the streamed levels is about 3-4 times faster (here: 3 minutes instead of 10):
  - the 150 m margin of the open-air map was computed square by square (44 million steps per try); now by rows and
    columns, and the nearest tile only for the pieces that need it;
  - when two maps did not fit together in memory, every try rebuilt both levels and every map from scratch; now each
    map is kept once built, and a new cut is first checked with the sizes already known (estimated by weight for the
    new pieces) before building anything.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 55 (2026-10-29)

  game does (MapLoad_Update with each record's neighbour request and the "must be loaded" flag, Map_MakeCurrent
  when you step on the other map's floor, the draw list with the flag blocks) and compares every view with the

- What it showed and what changed:
  - The visibility groups of rev 54 left up to half the screen empty in some rooms: off. Everything of the two

  - The room-sized maps of rev 53 asked for the next room too late: 14 forced waits in 22 loads with a slow disc.

    5): 2 forced waits in 10 loads, holes 0.2 % of the screen on average.
  - No more 1.5 MB of zeros padding each map up to 099's original layout: shorter loads (the link corridor is now
    2 MB instead of 3.4).
- How KFIV does it (checked on its maps): each map is a big area (3-6 MB) and only 1 to 5 records, the ones of
  the connecting corridor, ask for the neighbour, a few of them forcing it; most records ask for nothing.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 54 (2026-10-29)
------------------------------------
- Found why the neighbouring map never showed even when loaded: KFIV draws only what the floor you stand on can
  see. Each record has a group (+0x38) and each map one 0x40-byte block per group: the groups of that map seen from
  it (256 bits) and up to 16 (map number, group) pairs of the neighbouring map (Map_TestFlagBlockBit, used by the
  record draw list, objects and enemies). The streamed pieces carried the blocks of 099 unchanged, whose pairs
  name maps 98, 8 and 5, so the loaded neighbour was never drawn until you stepped on it.
- Every piece now has its own groups (the geometry chunks, about 20 m each) and blocks, as KFIV does: from each
  group you see what you can reach walking within 45 m, in the piece and through its doors into the neighbour (the
  next rooms, not the floor above or what is behind the rock); outdoors, the whole piece. Fewer things drawn
  indoors, so better frame rate too.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 50 (2026-10-28)
------------------------------------
- Streamed levels cut the way KFIV cuts its own maps (rev 48-49 cut them in straight slices, which split the
  open-air shore of level 0 in three: from the middle part one side was always missing, and the secret walls whose
  tile had no other geometry fell in no part and were lost).
  - The open-air area of a level (all of it, with the sea and cliffs around it) is never cut: it is one map. Indoor
    areas are grown from it layer by layer (walking distance) and cut where the passage to the next layer is
    narrowest; what is left behind is split into branches, each with its own entrance.
  - Every piece of geometry goes with the floor tile it faces (a wall with the room that sees it, not with the rock
    behind it) or, if it faces no floor, with the nearest floor tile. Objects, enemies, people, fountains and save
    stones go with the nearest floor tile, so none is lost.
  - Each map also carries a copy of the geometry within 7.5 m around it (facades seen from the shore, the start of
    the next room), so the neighbour not being loaded yet does not show as empty space at the border.
  - Level 0: 3 maps (the shore is 099), level 1: 5 maps, plus the corridor that links them; largest pair 11.2 MB
    of 11.5.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 48 (2026-10-28)

  - Each record asks for the neighbour you reach first by walking (floor path to the shared tiles); the tiles next

  - The maps take free map entries of the DAT (037, 036, 034, 033, 032, 044...; never the ones the executable
    treats specially) and, if there is no room, the end of the ISO below 4 GB.
- If something does not fit (no matching corridor, no free entries or no room on the disc) the build goes back
  to one map per level with the fade, and says why. --con-fundido forces that old mode.

Alpha 0.1 - revision 40 (2026-10-28)
------------------------------------
- The King's Field AI is now general and also used by the skeletons, green slimes and Head Eaters. Each one is
  set up like the KFIV monster it is built on (hits taken, death, walking, animations) and then decides like
  King's Field, with the weights and distances of its level 0 class (engine/kf1_ia_datos.py):
  - idle, wander (walks to random points around where it appeared), move towards you (from its start distance;
    keeps following up to a longer one), guard and up to three melee attacks;
  - each decision every possible action draws a random number up to its weight and the highest wins; a melee
    attack weighs less when you are farther than where it lands, and moving weighs double right after an attack;
  - after an attack, or when you are too close, it backs away if its KFIV model has a walk-back animation
    (otherwise it stays facing you).
- Skeletons: wander, come at you from 16.5 m, guard when you swing at them from close, double slash and thrust
  with King's Field reach and angles (never less than what the KFIV soldier had). Green slimes: bite all around.
  Head Eaters: bite from 4.8 m and back off.
- Kraken also has its King's Field idle action now; baby Krakens whip with their real weights (100, 100, 80).
- More King's Field action types decoded in docs/kf1_ia.md (wander, the skeleton's double slash, thrust and
  guard, how moving backs away).
- Baltails and the fish keep the KFIV bat and Seafang AIs (they fly or swim).
- Without the known executable each monster falls back to the AI of its KFIV base monster.

Alpha 0.1 (2026-10-26)
----------------------
First public release.

Map generator / King's Field port
- Ports King's Field levels into King's Field IV (PAL, SLES-50920), replacing map 099 (New Game).
- Real King's Field geometry and textures, with collision built from the models.
- Decor imported from the original MO.T models: barrels, tables, tombs, lamps, the healing fountain.
- Secret walls with the original King's Field model; doors as a rising slab with the original door texture.
- Healing fountain: HP only, with KFIV's own fountain script and effect.
- Breakable barrels; torches and lamps add a soft warm light; bluish fountain room.
- Snake damage capped at about 10 per hit (ported map only).

HUD and gameplay patch
- HP number inside the orb.
- Cross HUD: magic on top (icon, name, MP cost), consumable at the bottom (icon and count).
- Weapon EXP bar and level in the equipment screen; weapons level up at 70 and 140 hits.
- Optional twin-stick controls; starting gear in engine\equipo_inicial.txt.
- PARCHE_HUD.bat: HUD and/or controls patch for your own ISO, without the map generator.

Known issues
- Secret walls and doors are new: report anything that looks off.
- An enemy between you and an object takes the X button (the game's own priority).
